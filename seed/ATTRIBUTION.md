# Seed data attribution

The seeded meetings are real recordings under Creative Commons licenses. `seed/deepgram/*.json` are
machine transcripts of them (Deepgram nova-3, diarized), and the AI notes generated from them are
adaptations. Each is shared under the same license as its source recording.
